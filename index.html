About T.S. Tharun Paints

Your Trusted Partner for Paints & Hardware Supplies

T.S. Tharun Paints is a paint and hardware retailer located on Tirunelveli Road in Sankarankoil, Tenkasi District, Tamil Nadu. We serve homeowners, painters, contractors and builders with a range of products for painting, renovation and construction projects.

Our product range includes enamel paints, industrial and automotive paints, lacquers, insulating varnishes, PVC and hose pipes, plastic pipe fittings, hex bolts and other hardware essentials.
